Mentorship

Longer-term guidance for those deepening their own relationship with plants, ritual, and care.

— The Work

Some seasons ask not for a single session, but for a companion over time. Mentorship is a relationship — a slow, tended path for those called to deepen their own practice of the ritual arts of living in relationship.

Together we move at the pace of real learning: with the plants, the seasons, the kitchen, and the body as teachers. Each mentorship is shaped around the person, their questions, and the life they are living.

— What It Can Hold
