Recipes

Ancestral foods and kitchen medicine — nourishment as a daily act of care, memory, and remembrance.

— Food as Medicine

Food is among the oldest forms of medicine. In the kitchen, care becomes ceremony — a pot simmering, an herb steeped, a meal made slowly and shared at a table.

These recipes return the kitchen to its place as a site of healing: seasonal, culturally rooted, and offered in relationship with the body and the land. Not rules to follow, but ways of remembering.
